A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s clear
things up.

01Where do we start?

Start with a conversation about your business. Anthony can discuss how you accept payments today, the costs you want to understand, and the workflow you would like to improve. A recent processing statement is useful for that conversation.

02Can you help me understand my processing fees?

A payment review starts with your processing volume, fees, and current setup. The calculator on this site shows your effective rate and lets you explore a hypothetical comparison. A tailored proposal requires a review of your actual business details.

03Do I need to replace my current equipment?

That depends on your equipment, processor, and requirements. Share your current setup with Anthony before deciding on a change. Equipment compatibility and integrations need to be confirmed individually.

04Are the checkout and calculator real offers?

They are interactive examples. The checkout does not process payments, and the calculator does not quote FAST pricing or guarantee savings. Pricing, equipment, funding, and integrations require review and confirmation.
